Privacy Policy

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

Motomart is a marketplace app for buying and selling motorcycles, parts, riding gear and accessories in Egypt. It is run by Omar Amin ("we", "us").

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to send your sign-in code. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the email address Apple shares with us (Apple lets you hide your real one) and, the first time, your name.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, your governorate if you set one, and your language and theme choices.
  • Your mobile number, if you add one to sell. It is stored separately from your profile.
  • Your listings: title, description, price, category, item details, the governorate and area you choose, and photos.
  • What you do in the app: listings you save, people you block, reports you send, and a record each time a buyer asks for a seller’s number to message or call them (who asked, for which listing, how, and when). We also count listing views and shares; when you are signed in, a view or share is linked to your account.

Photos are re-encoded on your phone before they are uploaded. This removes the camera information stored in the file, including the GPS location where the photo was taken.

The app keeps your sign-in session, language, theme and chosen governorate on your phone.

We don’t collect your contacts or your device’s location. The app has no advertising networks, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. Featured listings (see our terms) are chosen by us and use no data about you.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your listings to buyers and let them contact you on WhatsApp or by phone call, as you choose for each listing.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: reviewing a new seller’s first listing, acting on reports, and limiting abuse such as collecting sellers’ numbers.
  • To show you your own listings, saved listings and view counts.
  • To run the service and fix problems.

We don’t sell your data and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Anyone using the app, signed in or not, can see your listings (including photos and the area you chose), your display name and the month you joined.
  • Your number is never shown in a listing. A signed-in buyer receives it only when they tap WhatsApp or Call on one of your live listings (only the ones you turned on for that listing), which opens a WhatsApp chat with you or their phone’s call screen. Each request is recorded and limited per hour.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Our moderators can see listings, reports and the record of moderation decisions.

Companies that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Resend, which delivers sign-in codes by email.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple.

When you contact a seller, the conversation happens in WhatsApp, under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy, or on an ordinary phone call. Either way the seller sees your number.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. A listing runs for 30 days unless you renew it. Sold and expired listings stay in your account, and open from a direct link, until you delete them.

When you delete your account (Account → Delete my account), we delete your profile, mobile number, listings and photos, saved listings, blocks, reports you sent and reports about you, and the record of number requests. Listing view and share counts are kept without anything that identifies you. We keep the ID of the deleted account, with no name, email or number, to finish removing its photos.

Backup copies of the database are kept for up to 30 days, then replaced.

Your choices

  • See and change your profile and your listings in the app at any time.
  • Change or remove your mobile number in your profile. Without a number, buyers can’t contact you and you can’t publish new listings.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete my account. It takes effect straight away.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us anything about this policy, at support@motomarteg.com.

Children

Anyone can browse listings without an account, and browsing without an account collects no personal information. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we don’t knowingly keep personal information about anyone younger. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us at support@motomarteg.com and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access rules in our database let each person change only their own data, and a seller’s number is released only through a recorded, limited request. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, tell us at support@motomarteg.com.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update it here and change the date at the top.
