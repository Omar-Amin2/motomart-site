Listing rules

  • List only items you own or are allowed to sell, located in Egypt, priced in Egyptian pounds.
  • Be honest: real photos of the actual item, and an accurate condition, year, mileage and licence status. Say clearly if a vehicle has no papers.
  • One item per listing. A set sold together counts as one item.
  • Buyers contact you through the WhatsApp button. Don’t put phone numbers, links or other contact details in titles, descriptions or photos.

Not allowed:
