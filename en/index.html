Motomart support

Buy and sell motorcycles, parts, riding gear and accessories in Egypt. Questions, problems or a listing to report: support@motomarteg.com.

Signing in

Enter your email and we send a 6-digit code; it can take a minute, so check your spam folder too. On iPhone you can also use Sign in with Apple or Google. Motomart has no passwords.

Selling

Tap Sell, choose a category, add 1 to 10 photos (and a video of up to 60 seconds if you like) and the details. Buyers can always chat with you in Motomart; you can also let them reach you on WhatsApp (by your number or your WhatsApp username) or by phone, at a time that suits you. A new seller’s first listing is reviewed before it goes live. Listings run for 30 days; renew, edit, mark as sold or delete them in My listings.

Buying safely

Keep the conversation in Motomart chat, meet in a public place, check the chassis and engine numbers against the licence, and never send money in advance. Report a listing from its page (Report), and a chat or a user from the chat’s ⋯ menu, where you can also block them; unblock them in Account → Blocked users.

Deleting your account

Account → Delete my account. Your profile, mobile number, listings, photos and videos are deleted straight away. If you can’t open the app, email support@motomarteg.com from the address you sign in with.
